# Market Pulse 2026-04-19

Driver  
Sharp unwind in oil after headlines around Strait of Hormuz reopening → geopolitical premium coming out fast, but still fragile underneath.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.71 (−7bp), US 10Y 4.25 (−7bp) — rally across the curve, slight bull flattening as front-end reacts more to easing stress.  
USD: DXY 98.23 (+0.05%) — stable, no strong safe-haven demand despite moves elsewhere.  
FX: EURUSD −0.16%, USDJPY −0.33%, USDCNH −0.09% — USD mixed, JPY a bit stronger with lower yields.  
Equities: SPX +1.20%, VIX 17.5 (−2.6%) — clear risk-on, vol keeps grinding lower.  
Commodities: Oil 84.0 (−9.9%), Gold +1.0% — oil collapses on de-escalation headlines, gold still bid.

WSJ/FT Insights  
Markets reacting to potential reopening of Hormuz, but actual flows remain uncertain. At the same time, Fed sounding cautious on inflation risks if energy shocks persist, while IMF highlights that damage from the shock is uneven and already hitting weaker economies.

Interpretation  
This is a classic unwind of stress positioning driven by oil — not a clean macro shift. Rates rally + equities up + VIX down all point to de-risking being reversed. But the fact that gold is still bid and USD isn’t selling off aggressively shows the market isn’t fully convinced. Feels more like relief than conviction.

Conclusion  
Market is pricing a de-escalation scenario, but still fragile. If oil stabilises lower, risk can keep grinding higher. If headlines reverse or flows don’t normalise, this unwind can flip back quickly.
