# Market Pulse 2026-06-23

Driver
Markets are still in a risk-on regime, but the move is less clean. Equities remain high, the dollar is strong, and oil is no longer falling sharply despite the ceasefire.

Transmission
Rates: US 2Y 4.19 (-4bp), US 10Y 4.49 (-2bp) — bull flattening, with the front end easing faster.
USD: DXY 101.14 (+0.14%) — dollar still strong and now clearly above 101.
FX: EURUSD lower, USDJPY slightly lower, USDCNH higher — FX is calm, but the dollar remains supported.
Equities: SPX -0.37%, VIX 19.9 (+14.9%) — equities pause and volatility jumps, showing more demand for protection.
Commodities: Oil 73.1 (-1.3%), Gold -2.2% — oil is still easing, while gold is losing safe-haven demand.

WSJ/FT Insights
The market is still adjusting to lower Middle East risk, but the physical recovery is not instant. Fertiliser prices have fallen back as traders look past the disruption, yet demand destruction and delayed shipments show the shock is still working through the system.

At the same time, SpaceX’s sharp reversal shows that high-rate pressure can quickly hit expensive AI and tech valuations.

Risk
The risk is that markets are treating the ceasefire as a full reset while parts of the energy and shipping system remain fragile. If rates stay high and AI valuations keep correcting, today’s risk-on tone could become more unstable.
