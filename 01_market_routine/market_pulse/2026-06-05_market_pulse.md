# Market Pulse 2026-06-06

Driver

The market shifted into risk-off. Equities sold off, volatility jumped and the dollar rallied as investors focused on inflation and policy risks despite lower oil prices.

Transmission

Rates: US 2Y 4.15 (+10bp), US 10Y 4.53 (+6bp) — bear flattening, with the front end repricing higher rates.
USD: DXY 100.07 (+0.64%) — strong dollar move on higher yields.
FX: EURUSD lower, USDJPY higher, USDCNH higher — broad dollar strength.
Equities: SPX -2.64%, VIX 21.5 (+39.7%) — risk sentiment deteriorated sharply.
Commodities: Oil 90.2 (-2.9%), Gold -3.3% — lower oil, but markets focused on rates and growth concerns.

WSJ/FT Insights

The oil market is still relying on inventory drawdowns and strategic reserves. FT analysis suggests that if inventories continue falling, oil prices could move significantly higher again.

At the same time, renewed tariff discussions and concerns about Treasury-market stability are keeping inflation and macro risks alive.

Risk

The risk is that markets are underestimating how persistent inflation could be. If oil rebounds or yields continue rising, today's correction may not be the end of the repricing.
