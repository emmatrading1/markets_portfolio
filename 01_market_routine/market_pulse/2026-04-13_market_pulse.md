# Market Pulse 2026-04-13

Driver  
Markets move back to an oil-driven setup as US-Iran talks fail and blockade risk around the Strait of Hormuz pushes crude back above $100, bringing inflation concerns back into play.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.81 (+~2bp), US 10Y 4.33 (+~3bp) — slight bear flattening, with the front-end reacting more to renewed inflation risk.  
USD: DXY 98.9 (+0.22%) — dollar firmer, moving back toward ~100 on uncertainty and higher rates.  
FX: EURUSD −0.23%, USDJPY +0.27%, USDCNH +0.07% — USD a bit stronger overall, JPY supported by yields, CNH stable.  
Equities: SPX −0.11%, VIX 21.3 (+10.8%) — equities slightly softer, VIX picking up but not at stress levels.  
Commodities: Oil 103.8 (+8.6%), Gold −0.5% — oil back above $100 on supply risk, gold down as rates and USD move higher.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is starting to price a more lasting disruption around Hormuz, with potential loss of Iranian supply adding to already tight conditions. This is also showing up in other commodities like aluminium, pointing to broader supply pressure. At the same time, the rebound in oil weakens last week’s “inflation cooling” narrative and pushes rate cut expectations further out.

Risk  
Markets are still in a bit of a transition phase. Oil back above $100 keeps inflation pressure alive and limits room for rate cuts. If tensions escalate further, oil could move higher again and drive a more clear risk-off move. If not, the market likely stays in this waiting mode.
