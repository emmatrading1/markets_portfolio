WSJ/FT Insights  
The move in oil over the past weeks has been messy, with a lot of players caught on the wrong side early on, especially in physical flows. At the same time, volatility is cooling off now, which usually happens once the initial stress passes, as long as things don’t escalate again.

Risk  
Feels like the market is moving out of the stress phase but not fully back to normal. Oil below $100 helps a bit, but levels are still high enough to keep inflation in the picture. If things stay calm, equities can grind higher. If oil spikes again, we’re probably back into volatility quickly.
