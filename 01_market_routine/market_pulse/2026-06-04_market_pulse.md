# Market Pulse 2026-06-05

Driver  
The market is still leaning risk-on. Oil is below $100 again, rates are easing, and equities keep pushing higher. But the energy story is not gone — it is just being priced as manageable for now.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.05 (-4bp), US 10Y 4.48 (-2bp) — bull steepening, with the front end easing more.  
USD: DXY 99.41 (-0.14%) — dollar softer on lower yields, but still firm in level.  
FX: EURUSD higher, USDJPY slightly lower, USDCNH lower — FX is calm overall.  
Equities: SPX +0.51%, VIX 15.4 (-4.1%) — strong risk tone, very little demand for protection.  
Commodities: Oil 93.1 (-3.2%), Gold +1% — oil is lower, but gold still has some safety bid.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is treating the oil shock as less urgent, but the structural issue is still there. Gulf states are already discussing pipelines to bypass Hormuz, which shows that energy security is becoming a long-term macro theme.

At the same time, low oil inventories and central bank warnings mean the inflation risk has not disappeared.

Risk  
The risk is that equities are pricing too clean a scenario. If oil rebounds or central banks keep pushing back against inflation, today’s low-volatility risk-on mood could look too complacent.
