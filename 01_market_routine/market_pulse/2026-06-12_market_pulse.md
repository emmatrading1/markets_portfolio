# Market Pulse 2026-06-12

Driver  
The market moved back into a clearer risk-on regime. Oil fell sharply, the equities recovered, and VIX dropped back below 18. But the geopolitical situation is still unstable, with Trump escalating threats around Iran’s oil infrastructure.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.09 (+3bp), US 10Y 4.48 (+2bp) — mild bear flattening, with the front end moving slightly more.  
USD: DXY 99.81 (+0.13%) — dollar firmer, but still below the 100 level.  
FX: EURUSD higher, USDJPY lower, USDCNH lower — FX is calm, with some softer dollar tone outside DXY.  
Equities: SPX +0.50%, VIX 17.7 (-9.1%) — risk appetite improved and protection demand fell.  
Commodities: Oil 84.3 (-2.5%), Gold +0.2% — oil relief is the main cross-asset driver.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is reacting more to falling oil than to the geopolitical headlines. Even with Trump threatening to take control of Iranian oil assets, crude moved lower, suggesting that investors are leaning toward a deal/reopening scenario.

The ECB’s rate hike shows central banks are still buying insurance against inflation, while European defence stocks show that war-related trades are no longer automatically rewarded.

Risk  
The risk is that markets are again pricing a clean resolution too quickly. If Iran tensions re-escalate or the ECB/Fed stay hawkish, today’s oil-led more risk-on optimistic regime could fade.
