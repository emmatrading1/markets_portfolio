# Market Pulse 2026-06-16

Driver  
The market is still in risk-on mode. Oil levels keep on falling after the US-Iran deal, US rates are easing, and equities are getting higher again. But bonds have less of a momentum than stocks, which shows that the market is not treating the ceasefire as a completely clean reset.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.05 (-2bp), US 10Y 4.45 (-3bp) — bull flattening, with the long end easing slightly more.  
USD: DXY 99.58 (-0.08%) — dollar is softer, but still not weak.  
FX: EURUSD higher, USDJPY flat, USDCNH lower — FX is calm overall.  
Equities: SPX +1.65%, VIX 16.1 (-0.7%) — strong risk-on, with volatility staying low.  
Commodities: Oil 78.9 (-2.8%), Gold +0.8% — oil is now below $80, while gold still has some reserve/safety support.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is celebrating the ceasefire and the reopening path for Hormuz, but Treasuries are more cautious. The deal reduces the energy shock, but it does not remove the inflation risk or the uncertainty around the Fed.

Gold also remains important in this regime. Central banks are still repatriating and diversifying gold reserves, which shows that long-term trust in the global reserve system is still being questioned.

Risk  
The risk is that equities price the ceasefire too quickly as a full back to normal regime. Oil is falling, but the physical reopening may take time, and the Fed may still have to stay hawkish if inflation remains strong.
