# Market Pulse 2026-05-19

Driver  
The market is still trading the oil shock, but the focus is now shifting towards the disconnect between bonds and equities. Rates keep pushing higher while equities remain close to record highs.

Transmission  
Rates: Slight bear flattening today — US2Y moved slightly more than US10Y. Yields still grinding higher overall, reinforcing the higher-for-longer inflation narrative.  
USD: DXY close to 100 again — dollar strength continues as higher yields and tighter financial conditions support the move.  
FX: USDJPY still mainly following the rates story. USDCNH stabilised after the volatility linked to the Trump–Xi summit. Broader FX still relatively calm.  
Equities: SPX only slightly lower despite bond pressure, while VIX moved back below 18 — still very little real panic or aggressive hedging demand.  
Commodities: USOIL stable above 103, gold slightly weaker — oil remains the key macro variable.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The interesting theme today is the growing divergence between bonds and equities. Bond markets continue pricing inflation and tighter financial conditions, while equities are still behaving as if growth and earnings can absorb the shock.

FT also highlighted how concentrated the rally has become, with AI and megacap tech continuing to drive equity strength even while rates keep rising in the background.

Risk  
The risk is that markets become too comfortable with structurally high oil and rising yields at the same time. As long as earnings stay strong, equities can ignore rates. But if yields continue grinding higher while oil stays above $100, tighter financial conditions could eventually hit valuations much harder.
