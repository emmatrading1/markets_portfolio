# Weekly Market Pulse — Week of June 1–7, 2026

The main story this week was the shift from oil relief to inflation concerns.

Oil moved lower toward $90, but investors became increasingly focused on inflation, central-bank tightening and whether markets have become too optimistic.

Energy

Oil continued to ease.

Lower prices helped calm markets, but inventories remain under pressure and the supply shock has not fully disappeared. The market is still relying on reserve releases and hopes of eventual normalisation.

Rates

Rates moved higher again.

Markets are increasingly pricing the possibility that inflation remains more persistent than expected. The ECB looks set to hike, while the Fed remains cautious.

Equities and volatility

Equities finally lost momentum.

SPX pulled back after weeks of gains and VIX moved sharply higher. AI remains the dominant theme, but investors are starting to question how much optimism is already priced in.

FX

Dollar strengthened.

Higher yields and inflation concerns pushed DXY back above 100. Broader FX remained relatively orderly despite the move.

Macro / flows

The market is still balancing two competing narratives.

On one side, lower oil supports growth and reduces inflation pressure. On the other, inflation remains elevated and policymakers are becoming more hawkish.

The FT also highlighted a broader bullish argument: AI, energy and defence spending could be creating a new global investment cycle that supports growth for years.

• Oil inventories and tanker flows
