# Market Pulse 2026-04-30

Driver  
Still oil, but slightly different tone today. Prices pulled back a bit, but the key shift is macro: central banks are all pausing, watching the same thing — energy-driven inflation. At the same time, the Fed transition (Powell → Warsh) is starting to creep in as a second layer of uncertainty for rates.

Transmission

WSJ/FT Insights  
Central banks are clearly in wait-and-see mode, but the bias is shifting. ECB and BoE are openly discussing hikes again if energy persists, even if they paused today. At the same time, the Fed transition introduces uncertainty: a more divided committee, less clear communication, and potential volatility in rates.

FX is starting to show cracks — the yen move is a reminder that policy divergence and intervention risk are real again.

Risk  
Two risks building in parallel:
1) Oil staying structurally high → forces central banks back into tightening  
2) Policy uncertainty (Fed transition + divided committees) → volatility in rates  

For now, markets are absorbing both without much stress, but that balance is fragile.
