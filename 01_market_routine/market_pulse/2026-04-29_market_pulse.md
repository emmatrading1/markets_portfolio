# Market Pulse 2026-04-29

Driver  
Oil remains the main driver. Back above $100 and now holding, which starts to matter more for inflation expectations and rates.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.86 (+~2bp), US 10Y 4.37 (+~2bp) — both moving higher, with the front-end slightly leading → bear flattening.  
USD: DXY ~98.7 — marginally higher, but still no meaningful safe-haven demand.  
FX: Overall quiet. USDJPY slightly higher in line with yields, rest of G10 relatively stable.  
Equities: SPX −0.5%, VIX ~18 — mild risk-off, but nothing significant.  
Commodities: Oil +4% (~103), Gold −0.7% — oil clearly dominating.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The situation is shifting from a short-term geopolitical move to something more persistent. Supply disruptions are real, and developments within OPEC (notably UAE tensions) add a more structural layer to the story.

At the same time, the impact is already visible in rates. Markets are starting to reprice inflation risk, while central banks remain cautious and wait for more clarity.

Risk  
What stands out is the limited reaction across other assets.

At these levels, oil would typically trigger a stronger adjustment — higher volatility, stronger USD, and a more pronounced move in equities. That is not fully happening.

This suggests that the market is still largely positioned for a resolution scenario, or at least not yet pricing the full implications of sustained higher energy prices.

If oil remains at current levels or moves higher, a broader repricing across assets would likely follow.
