# Market Pulse 2026-05-11

Driver  
Oil is still the main driver, but the market reaction is more controlled. Crude moved back up after Trump rejected Iran’s proposal, yet equities are still strong and VIX remains below stress levels. The market is not ignoring oil, but it is clearly less shocked by it now.

Equities: SPX +0.84%, VIX 18.1 (+5.4%) — equities strong, vol up but still below 20, so not stress.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is still very sensitive to Iran headlines, but less violently than before. Oil rises when peace talks look worse, but the rest of the market is not moving into full risk-off. The India article is important because it shows the energy shock is not just a commodity story anymore: it is hitting current accounts, currencies and equity flows in energy-importing economies.

Risk  
The risk is that the market gets too comfortable with high oil. For now, equities can still rally because investors are adapting to the shock and volatility remains contained. But if oil moves back above $100 and stays there, the pressure on inflation, EM flows and central banks becomes harder to ignore.
