# Market Pulse 2026-05-15

Driver  
Rates are the main story today. The market is starting to fully absorb the idea that inflation may stay sticky for longer, and global bond markets are selling off hard. Oil is still the background driver, but today the reaction is happening mostly through yields and the dollar.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.08 (+6bp), US 10Y 4.60 (+11bp) — clear bear steepening, with the long end moving more aggressively as markets reprice inflation and higher-for-longer risk.  
USD: DXY 99.3 (+0.4%) — dollar stronger again, helped by higher yields and tighter financial conditions globally.  
FX: EURUSD −0.38%, USDJPY +0.22%, USDCNH +0.40% — FX finally moving more noticeably. CNH weakness stands out and likely reflects sensitivity around the Trump-Xi summit and broader China concerns.  
Equities: SPX −1.24%, VIX 18.4 (+6.8%) — equities softer and volatility higher, but still not panic territory. Investors are cautious, not capitulating.  
Commodities: Oil 101.1 (slightly lower), Gold −2.4% — oil still elevated above $100, while gold drops sharply as real yields and the dollar move higher.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The important shift today is that the bond sell-off is becoming global. US Treasuries, gilts, JGBs and Bunds are all repricing at the same time as markets realise the energy shock may keep inflation elevated for longer. The market is no longer treating this as just an oil story.

The China angle also matters more now. CNH weakening and the focus on Trump-Xi discussions show that markets are watching whether China will help stabilise the Strait of Hormuz situation or instead use the crisis as geopolitical leverage.

Risk  
The risk is that markets lose confidence in bonds globally. So far, equities are holding relatively well considering the move in yields, and VIX is still below real stress levels. But if long-end yields keep rising this fast, financial conditions tighten everywhere at once. That is where the risk shifts from “oil shock” to broader pressure on growth, valuations and global liquidity.
