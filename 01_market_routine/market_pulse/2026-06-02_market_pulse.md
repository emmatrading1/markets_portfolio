# Market Pulse 2026-06-03

Driver  
The market's risk appetite is still strong. Oil is higher again near $93, but equities are not reacting much. The market is still more focused on liquidity, AI and IPO momentum than on macro stress.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.05 (+1bp), US 10Y 4.45 (flat) — slight bear flattening, with the front end reacting more.  
USD: DXY 99.21 (+0.03%) — dollar still firm, but not a major move.  
FX: EURUSD flat, USDJPY flat, USDCNH flat — FX is very calm overall.  
Equities: SPX +0.13%, VIX 15.8 (-1.7%) — equities remain strong, with almost no protection demand.  
Commodities: Oil 93.4 (+1%), Gold flat — oil bounced, but not enough to disturb risk sentiment.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market still has more greed than fear (cf. David Solomon). Even with oil elevated, Eurozone inflation rising and bond-market concerns in the UK, investors are still comfortable taking equity risk.

The bigger story is that AI and IPO liquidity are overpowering macro caution for now.

Risk  
The risk is complacency. If oil keeps rebounding, ECB/Fed hawkishness rises, or gilt stress returns, equities may eventually have to price the macro risks they are currently ignoring.
