# Market Pulse 2026-07-20

Driver  
Markets are turning more cautious as renewed US-Iran tensions push oil higher again. Equities are lower, long-term yields are rising and gold is slightly firmer, but volatility remains below 20, suggesting caution rather than full risk-off.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.18 (−0.05%), US 10Y 4.56 (+0.20%) — curve steepening, with the long end selling off while the front end is broadly stable.  
USD: DXY 100.72 (−0.04%) — dollar remains strong around 100, but shows little intraday movement.  
FX: EURUSD slightly higher, USDJPY flat, USDCNH lower — FX remains relatively calm overall.  
Equities: SPX −1.01%, VIX 18.5 (−1.6%) — equities weaken, but volatility remains contained below 20.  
Commodities: Oil 81.4 (−0.5%), Gold +0.1% — oil remains elevated after renewed tensions, while gold shows limited defensive demand.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The Iran ceasefire is becoming less credible as renewed attacks on tankers and fresh US strikes bring energy-supply risk back into focus. Brent moved above $90, showing that the market is rebuilding part of the geopolitical premium removed after the agreement.

At the same time, the Fed’s reduced use of forward guidance could make rate expectations and Treasury markets more volatile. China’s state-backed funds also stepped in to support domestic equities following the recent AI-led sell-off.

Risk  
The main risk is a renewed escalation around the Strait of Hormuz. Oil markets have not fully returned to normal, and further disruptions could lift inflation expectations, long-term yields and pressure equity valuations. For now, the market remains cautious rather than fully risk-off.
