# Weekend Macro Brief — May 30 2026

Driver  
The market shifted from oil panic to oil relief. Crude fell below $90, bonds rallied, and equities stayed very strong.

Macro Developments  
This week moved from de-escalation to risk-on.

Early week: oil moved back toward $90 and rates started calming down.  
Mid-week: equities kept grinding higher while FX stayed mostly quiet.  
End of week: oil fell below $90 on hopes of a Hormuz reopening, helping bonds and pushing stocks to new highs.

So overall: the market is no longer trading panic, but it may be pricing too clean a resolution.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil had its biggest relief move in weeks. The market is focused on tanker flows and a possible Hormuz reopening.

Rates: Yields moved lower as investors priced out the worst inflation scenario. But rates are still high versus pre-war levels.

FX: Dollar softened slightly as oil pressure faded. CNH and JPY stayed calm.

Equities: SPX stayed near record highs, supported by AI, semiconductors and low volatility. VIX fell close to 15.

Cross-asset: Lower oil is helping everything, but equities still look much more relaxed than bonds and policymakers.

Market Narrative  
The market is now asking: “Is the energy shock ending?”

For now, investors are choosing the positive answer: lower oil, lower inflation risk, stronger equities.

But the deeper risk is that the supply damage is not fully repaired yet.

Key Points to Watch  
• Oil staying below $90  
• Real tanker flows through Hormuz  
• Long-end Treasury yields  
• Fed and ECB rate expectations  
• AI/semiconductor breadth  
• Whether VIX stays below 16
