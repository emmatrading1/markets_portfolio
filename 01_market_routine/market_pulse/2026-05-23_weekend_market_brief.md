# Weekend Macro Brief — May 23 2026

Driver  
The market fully shifted from an oil shock story into a broader inflation and rates repricing. Bonds became the main stress point.

Macro Developments  
This week moved from stabilization to a fiscal and rates-driven repricing.

Early week: oil stabilized below panic highs, equities recovered.  
Mid-week: markets repriced sticky inflation and fewer Fed cuts.  
End of week: long-end yields surged again as deficit and sovereign debt fears intensified.

So overall: equities stayed resilient, but bonds kept deteriorating.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil stayed near the $100 regime. Markets increasingly believe prices may remain structurally higher.

Rates: Yields moved sharply higher, especially the long end. The story shifted from “higher for longer” toward “hikes may return”.

FX: Dollar stayed firm on higher yields. USDJPY tracked rates while CNH remained sensitive to trade-war and geopolitical headlines.

Equities: Equities resisted the bond sell-off, mainly driven by AI and megacap tech. Breadth remains narrow.

Cross-asset: Bonds are now the clear weak point. Oil was the original shock, but rates and fiscal stress became the transmission channel.

Market Narrative  
The market is no longer asking whether the Iran shock is temporary.

It is now asking: “What if geopolitical fragmentation and energy insecurity keep inflation structurally higher for years?”

That becomes more dangerous because it pressures:
* central banks
* sovereign debt markets
* valuations
* global liquidity

Key Points to Watch  
• Oil above or below $100  
• Long-end Treasury yields  
• Fed communication under Warsh  
• Equity weakness outside AI  
• US-China export controls  
• CNH and JPY volatility
