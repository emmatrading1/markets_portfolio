# Weekly Market Pulse — Week of June 15–21, 2026

The main story this week was the shift from energy relief to Fed-driven caution.

Oil fell hard after the US-Iran deal, but markets did not fully relax because Warsh’s first Fed meeting pushed rate-hike risk back into focus.

Energy

Oil moved sharply lower.

The reopening of Hormuz reduced the immediate shortage fear, and jet fuel prices also dropped. But the physical recovery is still slow: shipping confidence, insurance, storage, tanker flows and export logistics remain key constraints.

Rates

Rates moved back into focus.

The Fed held rates, but Warsh sounded hawkish and officials shifted toward possible rate increases. The market brought forward rate-hike expectations, especially after the dollar and front-end yields reacted strongly.

Equities and volatility

Equities stayed high, but the rally became less clean.

SPX remained around elevated levels, and VIX mostly stayed below 20. But AI and tech became more fragile as higher yields started to pressure expensive growth names, especially after SpaceX’s post-IPO reversal.

FX

Dollar strengthened.

DXY moved back above 100 and reached its strongest level in about a year. The dollar story is now less about safe-haven demand and more about US exceptionalism, AI investment and higher-for-longer Fed expectations.

Macro / flows

The market is no longer just trading the Iran deal.

Lower oil still supports risk appetite, but the next question is whether the AI boom is inflationary or productivity-enhancing. If AI adds demand before supply, the Fed may have to stay hawkish for longer.
