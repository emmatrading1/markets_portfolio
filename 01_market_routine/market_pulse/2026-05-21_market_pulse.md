# Market Pulse 2026-05-21

Driver  
Oil is finally easing a bit below $100, which is giving markets room to calm down. The focus is shifting away from immediate energy panic and back toward AI, earnings and broader growth expectations.

Transmission  
Rates: Slight bear flattening, with the 2Y rising a bit faster than the 10Y, but overall rates still relatively stable after the recent move higher.  
USD: DXY still firm around 99.2 — dollar strength remains supported by high yields and relatively tighter US financial conditions.  
FX: USDJPY following rates again, EURUSD nearly flat, USDCNH stabilising after the recent summit-driven volatility.  
Equities: SPX back above 7440 while VIX dropped below 17 — clear sign that investors are getting more comfortable with the current macro backdrop.  
Commodities: Oil fell back below $100 to around 97.7, helping sentiment significantly. Gold slightly lower with no real stress bid.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The important point today is that markets are increasingly treating the oil shock as manageable rather than catastrophic. Lower oil prices are allowing equities to refocus on AI, earnings and liquidity.

Another major theme is that equity strength is becoming extremely concentrated around AI-related names. Nvidia earnings, Korean semiconductors and AI infrastructure spending continue to dominate risk appetite globally.

Risk  
The risk is that markets become too complacent again. Equities are pricing a fairly optimistic scenario while rates remain elevated and geopolitical risks are still unresolved. If oil spikes again or yields resume moving sharply higher, the disconnect between bonds and equities could become a problem very quickly.
