# Weekly Market Pulse — Week of May 25–31, 2026

The main story this week was oil relief turning into full risk-on.

Crude fell below $90 on hopes of a Hormuz reopening, bonds calmed down, and equities kept pushing higher on AI strength.

Energy

Oil had a major relief move.

Brent posted its biggest monthly fall since 2020 as markets started pricing a possible reopening of Hormuz. But the energy shock is not fully over: inventories are still lower and supply normalisation could take time.

Rates

Rates eased as oil fell.

Markets priced out some of the worst inflation risk, but yields remain high compared with pre-war levels. The bond market is calmer, not fully comfortable.

Equities and volatility

Equities stayed very strong.

SPX kept making new highs and VIX moved lower, showing that investors are still not looking for protection. AI, semiconductors and earnings momentum remain the main support.

FX

Dollar softened slightly.

Lower oil reduced inflation pressure and took some support away from the dollar. Broader FX stayed calm, with no major stress in CNH or JPY.

Macro / flows

The market is now trading the positive version of the story: lower oil, lower inflation risk, stronger stocks.

But the FT articles show the risk: economists are less worried than in 2022, while equity valuations and AI optimism are becoming very stretched.
