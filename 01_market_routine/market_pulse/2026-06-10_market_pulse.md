# Market Pulse 2026-06-10

Driver  
The market seems to be still under pressure. Inflation is back at the center, rates moved higher, and equities weakened again as investors worry about Fed hawkishness and heavy AI equity supply.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.15 (+2bp), US 10Y 4.55 (+4bp) — bear steepening, with yields moving higher.  
USD: DXY 100.03 (+0.03%) — dollar holding firm around the 100 level.  
FX: EURUSD flat, USDJPY slightly lower, USDCNH higher — FX is calm but the dollar remains strong.  
Equities: SPX -1.62%, VIX 22.2 (+11.8%) — risk tone is weaker and protection demand is rising.  
Commodities: Oil 91.8 (+3.5%), Gold -4.5% — oil rebounded, while gold sold off sharply.

WSJ/FT Insights  
US inflation rose to 4.2%, confirming that the energy shock is still feeding into headline prices. Core inflation stayed more contained, but the Fed has less room to sound dovish.

At the same time, the AI trade faces a new pressure: SpaceX, Anthropic, OpenAI and large Big Tech share sales could increase equity supply and test how much demand is really left.

Risk  
The risk is that the market has to absorb a higher inflation, higher rates and huge AI issuance at the same time. If investors start selling existing tech winners to fund new IPO exposure, volatility could stay elevated.
