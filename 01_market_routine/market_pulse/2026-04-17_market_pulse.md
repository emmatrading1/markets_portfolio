# Market Pulse 2026-04-18

Driver  
Big move today comes from oil — sharp drop takes a lot of the geopolitical premium out. Market shifting out of stress mode.

Transmission

WSJ/FT Insights  
Market is reacting to de-escalation headlines, but the underlying situation isn’t fully fixed — flows and supply are still messy. At the same time, you start seeing positioning around other risks (private credit, funding, alternatives to Treasuries), so it’s not just oil anymore in the background.

Risk  
This looks like a transition out of the stress phase. Oil dropping helps a lot and unlocks risk-on. But it’s still headline-driven — if things turn again, oil and vol come back fast. For now, market is calmer, not stable.
