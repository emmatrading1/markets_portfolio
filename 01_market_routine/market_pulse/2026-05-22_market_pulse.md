# Market Pulse 2026-05-22

Driver  
Oil is still the key macro driver, but the market is getting more comfortable with the crisis regime. Crude is holding below $100, which gives equities room to rally and keeps volatility low.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.13 (+5bp), US 10Y 4.58 (+1bp) — clear bear flattening, with the front end reacting more to Fed/inflation risk.  
USD: DXY 99.3 (+0.1%) — dollar still firm, supported by higher-for-longer expectations.  
FX: EURUSD slightly lower, USDJPY +0.16%, USDCNH almost flat — USDJPY follows the rates/dollar story, broader FX is calm.  
Equities: SPX +0.59%, VIX 16.6 (-1.1%) — strong risk tone. Investors are not looking for protection.  
Commodities: Oil 97.2 (-0.8%), Gold -0.5% — oil below $100 helps sentiment, gold weak with no stress bid.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The key point is that markets are no longer trading the Iran crisis as a fresh shock. Oil is still the macro thermostat, but as long as it stays contained below $100, equities can focus on earnings, AI and liquidity.

The Fed story is less comfortable. Warsh is taking over while inflation risks remain high, and officials are becoming less willing to signal rate cuts.

Risk  
The risk is complacency. Markets are behaving as if the oil shock is manageable, but reserves are finite and peace talks remain uncertain. If oil spikes again or the Fed shifts more clearly toward hikes, this calm could reverse quickly.
