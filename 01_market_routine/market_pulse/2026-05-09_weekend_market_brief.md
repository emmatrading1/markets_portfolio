# Weekend Macro Brief — May 9 2026

Driver  
Oil was still the main driver, but the market started to price some relief again. A few Hormuz passages helped, especially LNG, but the situation is still fragile.

Macro Developments  
This week moved from stress to partial relief.

Early in the week: oil still high, rates under pressure.  
Later: Hormuz headlines improved, oil pulled back, equities recovered.

So overall: better tone, but not a clean normalisation.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil moved lower by the end of the week, from around $100+ back toward mid-90s. Still high, but less panic.

Rates: Yields stayed sensitive to oil/Fed risk. Cuts look less likely, and hikes are no longer impossible if inflation persists.

FX: Dollar softened. No strong safe-haven bid.

Equities: Equities held up well, VIX stayed low around 17. Risk appetite still alive.

Cross-asset: Relief in oil helped, but gold staying bid shows the market is not fully relaxed.

Market Narrative  
The market is trying to believe in partial resolution, not full peace.

Key Points to Watch  
• More Hormuz passages or renewed blockage  
• Oil holding below $100  
• Fed inflation reaction  
• Gold staying bid despite risk-on  
• Any stress in broader supply chains
