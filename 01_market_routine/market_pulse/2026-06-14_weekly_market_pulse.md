# Weekly Market Pulse — Week of June 8–14, 2026

The main story this week was the return of risk appetite after oil finally broke lower.

Markets started the week nervous about AI, rates and inflation, but ended stronger as oil fell on hopes of a US-Iran deal and investors absorbed huge AI-related fundraising.

Energy

Oil fell sharply.

Brent moved toward the mid-$80s as markets priced improving Hormuz flows and a possible US-Iran agreement. This helped reduce the immediate inflation scare, even if supply normalisation is not complete yet.

Rates

Rates calmed down.

Markets pushed back some expectations for rate hikes as lower oil reduced the worst inflation scenario. But the Fed, BoE and BoJ remain key risks next week.

Equities and volatility

Equities recovered.

The AI trade regained support after a volatile start to the week, and investors showed strong appetite for SpaceX, Anthropic and other large financings. But the amount of new equity supply is becoming a real test for the rally.

FX

Dollar remained important.

The dollar story is no longer just about rates. China’s mBridge project shows that payment systems and de-dollarisation are becoming a more strategic long-term theme.

Macro / flows

The market is still balancing relief and excess.

Lower oil supports risk appetite, but inflation is not fully solved and central banks cannot sound too dovish. At the same time, AI fundraising shows that liquidity is still strong, but also late-cycle.

What to watch next week

• Fed meeting and Warsh communication
• BoE and BoJ rate decisions
• Oil staying near the mid-$80s
• Hormuz tanker flows
• Treasury yields
• AI IPO and debt issuance demand
• Whether VIX stays below 20
