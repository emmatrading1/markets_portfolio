# Weekend Macro Brief — March 14 2026

Driver  
Escalation of the US–Iran conflict pushing oil higher and reviving global inflation concerns.

Macro Developments  
Energy markets dominated the week as fears of supply disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz drove sharp volatility in oil prices. Brent moved toward the $100 level as investors assessed scenarios ranging from temporary disruptions to a major supply shock.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil prices surged on geopolitical risk and uncertainty around Gulf supply routes.

Rates: Government bond markets sold off as traders priced fewer rate cuts and rising inflation risks.

FX: The US dollar strengthened on safe-haven demand and the US’s relative insulation as an energy producer.

Equities: Global equities turned more defensive, with energy-sensitive regions underperforming.

Market Narrative  
Markets are shifting from a “rate cuts” narrative to one driven by energy-led inflation risk. Oil has become the key macro variable guiding cross-asset moves.

Key Points to Watch  
• Brent crude approaching $100+  
• Stability of shipping through the Strait of Hormuz  
• Repricing of global rate expectations  
• Dollar strength versus energy-importing economies
