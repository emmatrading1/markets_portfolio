# Weekend Macro Brief — April 11 2026

Driver  
A fragile ceasefire around the Strait of Hormuz, but with real disruption still in place — keeping oil elevated and preventing a clean unwind of the inflation shock.

Macro Developments  
Markets moved from panic to relief this week, but not back to normal. Oil dropped and equities rebounded on the ceasefire, yet physical supply issues remain and inflation surprised higher — suggesting the shock is still feeding through.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil fell sharply early in the week, then stabilized around ~$95–100 as supply constraints and weak traffic through Hormuz persist.

Rates: Yields moved lower at first, then edged back up as inflation stayed firm and rate-cut expectations were pushed out.

FX: Dollar softened slightly as safe-haven demand faded, but moves remain secondary to rates.

Equities: Strong rebound, but still cautious and headline-driven.

Cross-asset: Clear tension between improving risk sentiment and ongoing stress in energy and inflation.

Market Narrative  
Markets are moving out of panic, but not back to normal. The shift is toward a “post-shock” phase — oil off the highs, but inflation and supply disruptions still in the system.

Key Points to Watch  
• Strait of Hormuz traffic and disruption  
• Oil stability vs renewed upside  
• Inflation and rate expectations  
• Start of earnings season  
• Early signs of credit stress (incl. private credit)
