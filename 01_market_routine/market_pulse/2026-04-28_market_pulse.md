# Market Pulse 2026-04-28

Driver  
Still the same driver: oil, but the tone is shifting again. Prices are pushing back toward $100 as talks stall, and supply disruption is clearly not resolving yet. What’s interesting is that markets are not reacting that negatively for now. We’re in that phase where the risk is visible, but not fully priced.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.82 (+~3bp), US 10Y 4.36 (+~2bp) — bear flattening, front-end moving more on inflation/rate risk.  
USD: DXY 98.65 (+0.16%) — slight bid, but no real safe-haven move.  
FX: EURUSD −0.16%, USDJPY +0.06%, USDCNH +0.17% — mild USD strength, but FX still quiet.  
Equities: SPX +0.12%, VIX 18.2 (+1.2%) — equities holding up, vol still low, no stress signal.  
Commodities: Oil 98.9 (+2.3%), Gold −1.2% — oil is the main move again, gold softer despite the geopolitical risk.

WSJ/FT Insights  
Oil is back near the key $100 area, and banks are starting to talk about much higher scenarios if Gulf flows do not normalise. Goldman’s higher oil forecasts matter because they show the market is no longer just dealing with a short headline shock, but with real supply uncertainty.

Central banks are still in wait-and-see mode. Inflation pressure is coming back through energy, but the growth outlook is also fragile, so they do not want to move too fast.

Risk  
The strange part is how calm the market still looks. Oil is almost at $100, but equities are stable, VIX is low, and FX is barely moving.

That can hold if investors keep believing in a near resolution. But if oil keeps pushing higher, the market may have to reprice quickly: higher yields, more volatility, and a more defensive tone.
