# Weekend Macro Brief — April 19 2026

Driver  
Oil repricing lower fast on Hormuz reopening headlines → market taking out the geopolitical premium, but still a headline-driven setup.

Macro Developments  
Clear shift this week from stress → relief. Oil dropped hard at the end, equities rallied, vol came down. But nothing is fully resolved — flows are still uncertain and inflation risk hasn’t disappeared. Feels more like an unwind than a real reset.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil went from high stress levels to a sharp drop (~$90 → low 80s) — mostly premium coming out, not clean supply recovery.

Rates: Yields down across the curve, slight bull flattening — less stress, but still not pricing aggressive cuts.

FX: Dollar not doing much — no strong unwind of safe-haven demand.

Equities: Strong bounce, VIX grinding lower — clear risk-on move.

Cross-asset: Not fully aligned — equities strong, but gold still bid and macro still unclear.

Market Narrative  
We’re moving out of the panic phase into a relief phase. But it’s not a “back to normal” market. It’s more: worst-case getting priced out, not fundamentals getting better.

Key Points to Watch  
• Whether Hormuz actually normalises (not just headlines)  
• Oil holding lower vs snapping back  
• Inflation reaction after the energy shock  
• Fed stance if inflation stays sticky  
• Any stress showing up in credit / funding
