# Market Pulse 2026-05-18

Driver  
Oil is still the main driver, but the market is starting to absorb the shock. The focus now is less “panic about supply” and more what high oil means for inflation, rates and growth over time.

Transmission  
Rates: Slight bear flattening today, but overall rates were fairly stable after last week’s heavy sell-off. Long-end yields still near global highs.  
USD: DXY still strong near 100 — the dollar continues benefiting from higher yields and tighter financial conditions.  
FX: USDJPY still mainly following the rates story. USDCNH moved more than usual again after the Trump–Xi summit. Broader FX still relatively calm.  
Equities: SPX slightly lower around 7408, VIX +3.4% but still below 20 — a bit more demand for protection, but still no real stress in the market.  
Commodities: USOIL back above 102, gold slightly higher — oil remains the key macro variable.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The important point today is that markets are getting used to structurally high oil prices. Bond markets still look uncomfortable, but equities are holding up surprisingly well.

Another interesting theme is that the physical energy situation looks slightly less catastrophic than feared a few weeks ago. Europe is increasing refinery output, using reserves and finding alternative imports, which is helping avoid full panic around shortages.

Risk  
The risk is that markets get too comfortable while yields keep creeping higher in the background. If oil stays above $100 and long-end rates continue moving up, tighter financial conditions could eventually hit equities and growth much harder.
