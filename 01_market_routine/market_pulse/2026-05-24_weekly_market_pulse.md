# Weekly Market Pulse — Week of May 18–24, 2026

The main story this week was the market getting used to the crisis regime.

Oil moved back below $100, which helped equities rally again, but bonds stayed under pressure as inflation and fiscal risks remained the weak point.

Energy

Oil eased from above $100 to around $97.

That gave markets room to breathe, but Hormuz and Iran headlines still matter. The crisis is not solved, only more absorbed.

Rates

Rates stayed the main risk.

The front end moved more as markets priced fewer cuts and even possible hikes under Warsh. Long-end yields also remain uncomfortable because of inflation, debt and bond vigilante fears.

Equities and volatility

Equities were strong again.

SPX moved back toward highs and VIX fell below 17, showing that investors are not looking for protection. AI and earnings momentum are still overpowering the macro stress.

FX

Dollar stayed firm.

USDJPY kept following the rates story, while CNH stabilised after the Trump-Xi volatility. Broader FX stayed calm.

Macro / flows

The market is no longer treating oil as a fresh shock.

The bigger question is whether high energy prices keep inflation sticky and force central banks to stay tighter for longer. At the same time, AI keeps supporting equities, which creates a clear gap between bonds and stocks.

What to watch next week

• Oil staying below or moving back above $100  
• Fed / Warsh reaction to inflation  
• US PCE data  
• Long-end Treasury yields  
• Whether VIX stays low  
• Whether AI momentum keeps supporting equities
