# Market Pulse 2026-04-27

Driver  
Still the same story: oil. Talks aren’t going anywhere, supply risk is still there, but markets aren’t reacting like they were a week ago. Feels like we’ve moved from panic → adaptation. People are starting to accept that oil stays high for a bit, while still thinking this probably ends at some point.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.79 (flat), US 10Y 4.32 (+~1bp) — slight bear steepening, long-end drifting higher on inflation risk from oil.  
USD: DXY 98.3 (−0.2%) — dollar softer, no real safe-haven demand anymore.  
FX: EURUSD +0.24%, USDJPY −0.10%, USDCNH −0.16% — small USD weakness, but honestly FX still very quiet.  
Equities: SPX +0.80%, VIX 19.1 (+2%) — equities bid, vol still under 20 → no stress, pretty constructive tone.  
Commodities: Oil 96.6 (+1.8%), Gold flat — oil still elevated, gold not really doing much.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The shift is subtle but important: market is no longer trading “will this escalate?” but more “how long does this last?”. Oil staying high shows the supply issue is real, but everything else suggests people still expect a resolution eventually. Central banks are basically waiting — inflation is picking up, but no one wants to react too early.

Risk  
Main risk is still oil. If it pushes back above $100, things probably move fast again (rates up, vol up). For now, market looks stable, but it’s a bit misleading — it’s pricing high oil without fully pricing what that means longer term. If the situation drags, that adjustment can come later.
