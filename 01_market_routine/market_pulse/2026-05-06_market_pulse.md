# Market Pulse 2026-05-06

Driver  
Clear relief move today. Oil dropped sharply after Trump paused the Hormuz escort operation and markets started pricing a better chance of negotiations with Iran instead of immediate escalation.

That was enough to push risk sentiment higher again.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.87, US10Y 4.35 — yields lower as oil eased.  
USD: DXY 98.0 (−0.5%) — dollar weaker, safe-haven demand fading.  
FX: EURUSD +0.5%, USDJPY −0.9% — broad USD weakness.  
Equities: SPX +1.1%, VIX 17.2 — equities strong, volatility still calm.  
Commodities: Oil 94.7 (−7.8%), Gold +2.9% — crude sharply lower, gold still supported by geopolitical uncertainty.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is still trading almost entirely through oil. As soon as crude pulled back, rates relaxed and equities immediately bounced.

At the same time, the supply story hasn’t disappeared. Opec tensions, limited shale response and ongoing Middle East uncertainty still keep the market cautious underneath the surface.

Risk  
Today was more risk-on, but not euphoric.

If negotiations keep progressing, oil can continue lower and equities probably grind higher. But the whole setup still looks fragile — one escalation headline could quickly reverse the move.
