# Market Pulse 2026-05-26

Driver  
Oil de-escalation is helping risk sentiment. Crude moved back toward $90, equities stayed strong, and volatility remained low. But rates are still the main macro pressure point.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.06 (-6bp), US 10Y 4.51 (-5bp) — mild bull steepening, but yields still structurally high.  
USD: DXY 99.06 (+0.06%) — dollar still firm.  
FX: EURUSD slightly lower, USDJPY +0.16%, USDCNH flat — FX relatively calm overall.  
Equities: SPX +0.37%, VIX 16.8 (+1.4%) — equities still very resilient.  
Commodities: Oil 92.7 (-4.4%), Gold +0.3% — lower oil after improved Hormuz shipping flows.

WSJ/FT Insights  
Markets are becoming less reactive to the Iran/Hormuz story as shipping gradually resumes. But central banks are still focused on second-round inflation effects from energy.

The macro story is slowly shifting from “oil shock” to “higher-for-longer rates.”

Risk  
Equities still look much calmer than bonds. If yields start rising again, stocks may eventually have to catch up to the rates repricing.
