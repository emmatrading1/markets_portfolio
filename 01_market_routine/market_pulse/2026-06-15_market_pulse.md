# Market Pulse 2026-06-15

Driver  
The market is now clearly risk-on after the US-Iran deal and the planned reopening of the Strait of Hormuz. Oil dropped sharply, rates eased, and equities pushed higher than last week.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.04 (-4bp), US 10Y 4.45 (-3bp) — bull steepening, with the front end easing more.  
USD: DXY 99.53 (-0.28%) — dollar softer as safe-haven demand and rate pressure faded.  
FX: EURUSD higher, USDJPY slightly lower, USDCNH lower — FX confirms a calmer macro setup.  
Equities: SPX +0.50%, VIX 16.8 (-4.9%) — strong risk tone, with volatility back below 20.  
Commodities: Oil 80.2 (-4.9%), Gold +2.8% — oil is repricing the deal, while gold keeps some hedge demand.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is pricing the US-Iran deal as a major relief for the global energy risk. Lower oil reduces inflation pressure and gives equities more room to rally.

But the reopening of Hormuz may take time. Even with a deal in place, energy markets are unlikely to return to normal overnight.

Risk  
The risk is that markets price the deal too smoothly. If Hormuz flows recover slowly or tensions return, today’s risk-on move could be too optimistic.
