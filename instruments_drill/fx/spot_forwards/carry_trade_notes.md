## FX CARRY TRADE LOGIC

### core idea
carry trades exploit interest rate differentials between currencies

investors borrow in low-yield currencies and invest in high-yield currencies

profit comes from the interest rate spread

### carry trade structure
borrow low interest rate currency  
convert into higher yielding currency  
invest in higher yielding assets

example

borrow JPY  
buy USD assets

profit ≈ interest rate differential

### forward premium / discount intuition
interest rate differentials determine forward pricing

higher rate currency → forward discount  
lower rate currency → forward premium

example

USD rate > EUR rate

USD trades at forward discount  
EUR trades at forward premium

this ensures no arbitrage between FX and money markets

### funding currencies
low yield currencies are often used to fund carry trades

typical funding currencies

JPY  
CHF

these currencies tend to weaken during risk-on periods as investors borrow them

### target currencies
high yield currencies attract carry flows

examples

AUD  
NZD  
emerging market currencies

investors buy these currencies to earn higher yields

### risk-on environment
when global risk appetite is strong

carry trades expand  
capital flows into high-yield currencies  
funding currencies weaken

result

AUD ↑  
EMFX ↑  
JPY ↓

### risk-off environment
when markets become stressed

carry trades unwind  
investors close positions  
funding currencies strengthen rapidly

result

JPY spikes  
high-yield currencies fall sharply

### carry trade risk
main risks

exchange rate volatility  
sudden risk-off shocks  
policy changes in interest rates

large FX moves often occur during rapid carry unwinds

### macro importance
carry trades are a major driver of FX flows

they link

interest rates  
global risk sentiment  
currency movements

understanding carry dynamics is essential for macro and FX trading
