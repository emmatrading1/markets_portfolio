# Market Pulse

This folder contains short daily notes on markets.

Each note is based on a couple of articles or macro releases from the day. The goal is simply to stay close to what is moving markets and to practice linking news to price action across assets.

Most notes look at things like:

- macro developments
- central bank expectations
- moves in rates, FX, equities or commodities
- shifts in market narratives

Weekend notes are slightly different and usually summarize the main themes from the week - a way to keep a consistent habit of following markets.
