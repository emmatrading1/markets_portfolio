# Market Pulse 2026-03-12

Driver
Escalating disruption in Gulf oil flows remains the dominant macro driver as attacks on shipping and energy infrastructure keep the Strait of Hormuz largely impassable. Despite strategic reserve releases and attempts to reroute exports, markets remain focused on the risk that the conflict prolongs a major supply shock.

FT Insights
The International Energy Agency warned that the Iran war has triggered the largest supply disruption in oil market history, with production cuts exceeding 10mn barrels per day as shipping through the Strait of Hormuz stalls. Although Gulf producers are rerouting exports and strategic reserves are being released, the scale and duration of the disruption remain uncertain, keeping energy markets highly volatile.

Risk
Further attacks on energy infrastructure or prolonged disruption to shipping routes could push oil prices significantly higher and intensify the global risk-off environment.
