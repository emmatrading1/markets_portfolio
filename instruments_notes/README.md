### Instruments Notes

This folder contains my personal notes while going through the different financial instruments used in markets.

The goal is simply to build strong intuition on how these products actually work: pricing logic, payoff structures, and how they are used in trading or structuring.

Most of the notes follow Hull's *Options, Futures and Other Derivatives*, combined with small exercises and examples.

The topics covered include:

- derivatives (options, futures)
- FX markets (spot, forwards, carry)
- rates products (bonds, swaps, yield curves)
- structured products
