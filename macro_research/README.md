### Macro Research

This folder contains longer macro notes where I try to go a bit deeper on specific themes affecting markets.

Unlike the daily *Market Pulse* notes (which are short summaries of current news), I focus on broader macro narratives — policy shifts, structural trends, or valuation dislocations and try to link them to potential market implications across assets (equities, FX, rates, commodities).

Topics so far include:

- China policy support and equity market repricing
- Upcoming note: FED policy path 

More notes will be added as new macro themes emerge.
