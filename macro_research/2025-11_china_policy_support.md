China Macro Pulse — Policy Support Starting to Show in the Data

Week of Nov 3–7, 2025  
Emma Abeelack

China has started to see the first signs of stabilization after several months of policy support. The combination of liquidity easing, fiscal measures and targeted consumption programs is beginning to show up in the data, even if the property sector remains a drag.

The Hang Seng is still trading around 8.5x forward P/E, well below historical averages, suggesting the market remains skeptical that stimulus will translate into a durable recovery.

Recent developments:

Oct 12 – PBOC cuts the reserve requirement ratio by 50bp, injecting roughly CNY 1tn of liquidity.  
The Hang Seng moved about +2.1% on the announcement while mainland equities rallied more strongly.

Oct 28 – The NPC approves a CNY 10tn local government debt swap program.  
The initial reaction was negative as investors were expecting fresh stimulus, but sentiment improved as the program reduces interest pressure on local governments.

Nov 1 – A CNY 300bn trade-in program for consumer goods (EVs and appliances) is announced.  
Consumer names reacted positively with Alibaba and JD both gaining on expectations of stronger retail activity.

Nov 3 – October data came in stronger than expected.

Retail sales: +4.8% YoY (vs +4.0% expected)  
Industrial production: +5.6% YoY  
Property activity remains weak, though the pace of decline has moderated.

The Hang Seng closed the session around 26,100 (+1.8%).

Overall the data suggests that stimulus is starting to feed through to consumption. Confidence indicators have also improved slightly, although the property sector is still a key source of downside risk.

Market implications

If consumption momentum continues, Chinese equities could see some rerating from current depressed valuation levels. Consumer platforms such as Alibaba and JD would likely benefit the most from trade-in incentives.

Banks may also see improved sentiment as the debt swap program reduces some of the pressure on local government balance sheets.

Key risks remain the property sector and geopolitical tensions, which could quickly reverse the improvement in sentiment.

Levels to watch

Hang Seng: support 24,500 / resistance 27,500  
USD/CNH: sustained move below 7.10 would signal improving confidence  
China 10Y yield: around 2.15%, continued declines would confirm ongoing easing
