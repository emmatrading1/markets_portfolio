**WEEKLY MACRO ANALYSIS**
China Growth Rebound: HSI Opportunity
Emma Abeelack | Week of Nov 3-7, 2025 | Markets
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**THESIS**
Beijing's CNY 10 trillion stimulus + stabilizing consumption (retail sales +4.8% vs +3.2% prior) = HSI mispriced at 8.5x P/E (40% below historical avg). Policy support gaining traction but market still pricing structural decline. Target: HSI 30,000 (+15%) as data confirms reacceleration.

**WHAT HAPPENED**
• Oct 12: PBOC cuts RRR 50bp → CNY 1tn liquidity
Market reaction: HSI +2.1%, A-shares +3.4%

- Oct 28: NPC approves CNY 10tn debt swap (reduces local gov interest burden CNY 600bn)
Market reaction: Initial sell-off (no "new money"), then recovery as debt risk falls
- Nov 1: Ministry of Commerce announces CNY 300bn trade-in program (EVs, appliances)
Market reaction: Consumer stocks rally (Alibaba +4%, JD +3.2%)
- Nov 3: October data beats
- Retail sales: +4.8% YoY (exp +4.0%) - 6-month high
- Industrial production: +5.6% YoY (steady)
- Property: Still weak (-5.2% MoM) but pace of decline slowing
Market reaction: HSI closes 26,100 (+1.8% on day)

**MACRO THEME**
Stimulus finally showing up in hard data. Consumer confidence rising (88.2 vs 85.1), trade-in program creating direct demand. Property still drag but not worsening. Market too pessimistic at 8.5x P/E with 5%+ growth.

**MARKET IMPACT (If I'm right)**
→ HSI: Rerate to 30,000 (11x P/E, still below 13x historical)
→ Consumer stocks: Alibaba, [JD.com](http://jd.com/) benefit directly from trade-in program
→ Banks: Debt swap reduces NPL risk, trading at 0.4x P/B (deep value)
→ Yuan: Stabilizes 7.10-7.20 (signals confidence returning)

**RISKS (If I'm wrong)**
→ Q4 GDP <4.8% (momentum stalls) → HSI retests 24,000 support
→ Property crisis deepens (contagion to financials) → exits required
→ Geopolitical shock (Taiwan) → immediate -20% HSI
