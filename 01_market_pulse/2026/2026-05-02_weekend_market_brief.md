# Weekend Macro Brief — May 2 2026

Driver  
Oil stayed the main driver, but the market reaction changed. Crude moved back above $100, yet equities kept holding up. So the week was less about panic, more about the market learning to live with high oil — for now.

Macro Developments  
This week was a strange mix: oil kept pushing inflation risk higher, central banks stayed cautious, and equities still managed to grind up, helped by tech/AI earnings.

So overall: macro stress is still there, but risk appetite has not broken yet.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil moved from the high 90s back above $100. Supply risk is still real, and the market is now focused on how long the disruption lasts.

Rates: Yields moved higher overall, especially at the front-end. The market is not pricing easy cuts anymore — inflation risk is back in the rate story.

FX: Dollar mostly stayed weak/flat. No real safe-haven bid, but USDJPY stayed important because of yen intervention risk.

Equities: Equities were surprisingly resilient. Tech/AI carried the market, while VIX stayed low. Not full risk-on, but clearly no panic.

Cross-asset: Mixed picture. Oil and rates say macro risk is building, but equities and vol say investors are still comfortable.

Market Narrative  
The market is not ignoring the energy shock, but it is not fully pricing the long-term damage either.

The key shift this week:  
→ oil above $100 is no longer treated as immediate panic  
→ but if it stays there, the pressure on inflation, rates and margins becomes harder to avoid.

Key Points to Watch  
• Oil staying above $100 or pulling back  
• Any real progress on Hormuz / Iran talks  
• Central banks: patience vs forced hikes  
• Whether AI earnings keep supporting equities  
• Yen / FX intervention risk  
• Any delayed repricing in equities if rates move higher
