# Market Pulse 2026-03-17

Driver  
Markets continue to be driven by the Middle East conflict and its implications for energy supply and inflation. The BIS warned that a prolonged disruption could push interest rates higher and weigh on asset prices, reinforcing the risk of tighter financial conditions globally. However, despite these risks, markets remain relatively orderly, suggesting investors are not yet pricing a full escalation scenario.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.680 (+0.19%), US 10Y 4.226 (+0.14%) — front-end yields rise faster than the long end, flattening the curve and indicating markets are repricing short-term inflation and central bank expectations rather than adding long-term risk premium.<br>
USD: DXY 99.808 (−0.01%) — the dollar remains broadly stable, reflecting a balance between higher yields (supportive) and steady risk sentiment (limiting safe-haven demand).<br>
FX: EURUSD +0.01%, USDJPY +0.09%, USDCNH +0.02% — limited moves across FX, with slight USD strength against JPY consistent with higher yields, while broader currency markets remain contained.<br>
Equities: SPX +1.01%, VIX 23.61 (+0.43%) — equities rebound despite macro risks, while volatility remains elevated, pointing to cautious positioning rather than full risk-on sentiment.<br>
Commodities: Oil 96.66 (+2.58%), Gold −0.04% — oil continues to climb toward $100 as supply risks persist, while gold remains stable as the lack of acute risk-off limits safe-haven demand.

FT Insights  
The BIS highlights the risk that persistent energy shocks could reverse expectations of rate cuts and tighten financial conditions, particularly as higher borrowing costs begin to expose vulnerabilities in credit markets and asset valuations.

Risk  
The current market resilience suggests investors are treating the geopolitical shock as contained. However, a prolonged or escalating disruption — particularly involving the Strait of Hormuz — could trigger a sharper repricing toward higher yields, tighter financial conditions and renewed pressure on risk assets.
