# Market Pulse 2026-06-18

Driver  
The market is still in relief mode, but the Fed has brought rates back into focus. Oil and jet fuel are falling as markets price the reopening of Hormuz, while equities remain strong and volatility is lower.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.18 (-1bp), US 10Y 4.46 (-3bp) — bull flattening, with the long end easing more.  
USD: DXY 100.84 (+0.45%) — dollar stronger as the Fed sounds more hawkish.  
FX: EURUSD lower, USDJPY higher, USDCNH slightly higher — FX confirms dollar strength.  
Equities: SPX +1.07%, VIX 16.5 (-10.5%) — strong risk-on tone, volatility back down.  
Commodities: Oil 75.7 (+1.0%), Gold -0.8% — oil stabilises after the sharp fall, while gold loses some safe-haven demand.

WSJ/FT Insights  
Jet fuel prices are falling sharply as traders expect Middle Eastern exports to restart. This gives relief to airlines, but prices are still above prewar levels and inventories remain depleted.

The oil market is increasingly looking past the Iran crisis. Traders are now more focused on possible oversupply than on the shortage fears that dominated earlier in the conflict.

The Fed stayed on hold, but Warsh’s first meeting was hawkish. Officials are now tilted toward possible rate rises if inflation remains too high, even after the fall in oil prices.

Risk  
The risk is that markets price the energy shock as over too quickly. Lower oil is positive, but physical flows, inventories and refined products are not fully normal yet. At the same time, a hawkish Fed could limit how far the equity rally can extend.
