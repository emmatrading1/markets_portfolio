# Market Pulse 2026-03-27

Driver  
Markets are driven by a persistent inflation-risk narrative as oil pushes higher and supply disruption risk remains unresolved. The focus is shifting from headlines to the durability of the energy shock, keeping the backdrop unstable and biased toward higher-for-longer pricing.

WSJ/FT Insights  
Oil remains the key macro driver. FT points to a material inflation risk (~4.2% US), reinforcing that the shock may persist. At the same time, pricing is partly shaped by US political intervention, creating a fragile and policy-sensitive environment.

Risk  
Further upside in oil or prolonged disruption would push inflation expectations and yields higher, pressuring equities and keeping volatility elevated. A credible easing would reverse the move, but the market still lacks conviction for a clean normalization.
