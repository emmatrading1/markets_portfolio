WSJ/FT Insights  
The dollar story is going back to US exceptionalism. Investors still see the US economy and AI trade as strong enough to keep the Fed from cutting quickly.

The market is pricing relief on energy, but the recovery from the Iran shock will take time. Oil flows, inventories and production will not normalise immediately.

Risk  
The risk keeps being that markets treat lower oil as a clean reset too quickly. The direction is positive, but the physical energy system is still fragile and the Fed may stay cautious if US inflation remains strong.
