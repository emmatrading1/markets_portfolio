# Weekend Macro Brief — March 28 2026

Driver  
A persistent energy shock driven by the effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz, pushing oil higher and reviving a global stagflation narrative.

Macro Developments  
Markets saw a rare simultaneous sell-off in equities and bonds as oil surged (~+50% since the start of the conflict). The shock is spreading beyond energy into shipping, fertilisers and broader supply chains, reinforcing concerns around both inflation and growth.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil driven by supply disruption and chokepoint dynamics; shipping constraints amplify the shock.

Rates: Yields rise across the curve, with higher term premium and inflation uncertainty.

FX: Dollar supported by higher yields, though moves remain secondary to oil and rates.

Equities: Broad risk-off, with diversification breaking down as both equities and bonds decline.

Cross-asset: “Nowhere to hide” — even gold sold off amid liquidation and higher real yields.

Market Narrative  
Markets are shifting into a stagflation regime where energy dominates. The key change is from cyclical inflation to a supply-driven shock, weakening growth while keeping inflation elevated and volatility high.

Key Points to Watch  
• Oil trajectory and supply disruption  
• Status of the Strait of Hormuz  
• Long-end yield / term premium  
• Spillovers into global supply chains  
• Signs of demand destruction or policy response
