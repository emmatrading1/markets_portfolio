# Market Pulse 2026-05-05

Driver  
Market finally got a bit of relief from oil. Crude dropped back below $100, and that was enough for equities to push higher again. So the tone shifted slightly more risk-on, but the macro backdrop is still inflation-heavy underneath.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.95 (flat), US 10Y 4.43 (−~1bp) — curve still flat overall, no real growth panic, but rates staying elevated.  
USD: DXY 98.5 (flat) — dollar still directionless.  
FX: EURUSD +0.12%, USDJPY −0.15%, USDCNH −0.04% — FX mostly calm, mild USD softness.  
Equities: SPX +0.81%, VIX 17.4 (−5%) — clear relief move in equities, vol fading again.  
Commodities: Oil 99.3 (−3.2%), Gold +0.3% — oil cooling helps sentiment materially.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is still trading the same logic: as long as oil stops accelerating higher, investors are comfortable leaning back into risk, especially US tech.

At the same time, the inflation story is spreading more broadly now. Companies are openly talking about passing higher costs to consumers, central banks outside the US (like Australia) are tightening again, and Europe looks increasingly exposed to the energy shock.

The UK bond market also stood out yesterday — gilts sold off hard as investors started pricing not just inflation risk, but political and fiscal stress as well.

Risk  
The setup still feels fragile underneath the surface.

Right now, lower oil = relief rally.  
But the underlying macro pressure has not disappeared at all. If crude turns back up or Hormuz tensions escalate again, markets probably go straight back into inflation/rates repricing mode.
