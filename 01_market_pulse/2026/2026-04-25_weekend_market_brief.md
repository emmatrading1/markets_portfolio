# Weekend Macro Brief — April 26 2026

Driver  
Market stuck around high oil (~$90–100) → not panic anymore, but still enough to keep inflation and macro pressure alive.

Macro Developments  
This week wasn’t a clean trend — more of a transition.

Early in the week: oil spikes → risk-off tone, yields up.  
Mid-week: ceasefire headlines → some relief, vol down.  
End of week: oil still high → market pauses, no full risk-on.

So overall: not stress, not relief — just stuck in between.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil stayed elevated all week (~90–97). No real normalization — just back-and-forth on headlines.

Rates: Yields moved slightly higher overall, with some bear flattening early, then small bull moves. Net: still pricing inflation risk, not cuts.

FX: Dollar weak to flat. Safe-haven demand fading, but no strong alternative either. FX mostly quiet.

Equities: Choppy. Some dips, some support, but no strong direction. VIX stayed below stress (~18–20) → controlled market.

Cross-asset: Mixed signals. Equities not panicking, but oil + rates still tight → macro not clean.

Market Narrative  
Market is no longer in panic mode, but also not comfortable.

The key shift vs last week:  
→ before: reacting to shock  
→ now: trying to price how persistent it is

So it’s a “wait and see” market, driven by oil headlines, not fundamentals.

Key Points to Watch  
• Oil: does it stay ~90–100 or break higher again  
• Real supply vs just headlines around Hormuz  
• Inflation prints reacting to energy  
• Central banks staying patient vs forced to react  
• Any stress in funding / liquidity (early signals starting)
