# Market Pulse 2026-05-13

Driver  
Oil is still in the background, but today the focus is inflation. CPI came in hotter, and it confirms that the energy shock is starting to move beyond crude. The market is not panicking, but the rates story is becoming less comfortable.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.98 (−1bp), US 10Y 4.46 (flat) — slight bull steepening, but yields are still high. The important point is higher-for-longer risk.  
USD: DXY 98.5 (+0.2%) — dollar firmer, helped by sticky inflation and fewer reasons to price Fed cuts.  
FX: EURUSD −0.27%, USDJPY +0.11%, USDCNH flat — FX still quiet, USDJPY slightly higher with the dollar/yield backdrop.  
Equities: SPX −0.16%, VIX 17.9 (−0.8%) — equities a bit softer, but no real stress. VIX still below 18, so protection demand remains low.  
Commodities: Oil 101.7 (slightly lower), Gold −0.4% — oil still above $100, gold softer as yields remain a headwind.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The key point today is that disinflation is fading. Inflation is not just an oil story anymore: fuel, airfares, food and some goods are starting to show pass-through. India raising gold tariffs shows the same pressure from another angle: energy-importing countries are trying to protect their currencies and external balances.

Risk  
The risk is that markets stay too calm while inflation gets worse. Equities are holding, VIX is low, and oil is slightly lower on the day, so the surface looks fine. But if inflation keeps moving higher while oil stays above $100, the Fed has much less room to sound dovish. That is where rates could become the real problem for equities later.
