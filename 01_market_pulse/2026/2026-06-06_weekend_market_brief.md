# Weekend Macro Brief — June 6 2026

Driver  
The market shifted from oil relief to macro uncertainty. Oil is lower, but yields, tariffs and inflation risk are back in focus.

Macro Developments  
This week moved from risk-on to a sharper repricing.

Early week: oil stayed lower and equities kept pushing higher.  
Mid-week: volatility stayed low despite inflation concerns.  
End of week: yields and the dollar rose, while equities finally corrected.

So overall: oil is less scary, but the market is not fully relaxed.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil moved toward $90, but inventories are still being drawn down.

Rates: Yields rose again as inflation risk stayed alive.

FX: Dollar strengthened and moved back above DXY 100.

Equities: SPX fell sharply and VIX jumped above 21.

Cross-asset: Lower oil helps, but rates and policy uncertainty are now driving stress.

Market Narrative  
The market is asking: “Is lower oil enough?”

For now, the answer is not clear. Oil relief helps, but tariffs, inventories and Treasury-market pressure keep the setup fragile.

Key Points to Watch  
• Oil near $90  
• Inventory drawdowns  
• Treasury yields  
• Dollar above DXY 100  
• Tariff headlines
