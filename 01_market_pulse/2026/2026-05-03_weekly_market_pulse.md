Week of Apr 28 – May 2, 2026

The main story this week was a growing disconnect. Oil moved higher again, clearly back above $100 at times, but markets didn’t really react. So macro pressure is building, while risk still holds.

Energy

Oil stayed elevated and volatile (~105–125 intraday).

This now looks more structural than just headlines — supply risk is real (Hormuz, OPEC tensions).  
Market is starting to price duration, not just shock.

Rates

Yields drifted higher overall.

Central banks held, but tone shifted: hikes back on the table if energy feeds into inflation.  
Fed transition adds another layer of uncertainty.

Equities and volatility

Equities stayed strong, driven by tech/AI.

VIX still low (~17–18) → no stress.  
So macro tightening, but equities not following.

FX

Mostly quiet, but some cracks.

Yen volatility stands out (intervention risk).  
USD not behaving like a strong safe haven.

Macro / flows

Energy shock becoming more persistent.  
OPEC cohesion weaker (UAE exit).  
US still attracting flows (AI), increasing concentration.

What to watch next week

• Oil staying >$100 or pushing higher

For now, market still absorbing it — but the balance is getting thinner.
