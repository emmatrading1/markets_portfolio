WSJ/FT Insights  
The gap is still there: geopolitics is escalating (Hormuz risk, direct threats), but markets are not reacting aggressively. US equities keep holding up, supported by earnings and structural flows, while the rest of the world looks more exposed to the energy shock.

At the same time, the macro pressure is building underneath — especially outside the US (Asia, Europe), where higher energy costs are starting to hit growth and inflation more directly.

Risk  
Market still looks stable, but it’s a very conditional stability.

If headlines stay contained, it probably holds.  
But if oil pushes higher again or the situation in Hormuz escalates materially, the current calm could break quickly — likely starting with rates and FX before equities catch up.
