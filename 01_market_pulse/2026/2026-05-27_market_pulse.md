# Market Pulse 2026-05-27

Driver  
The market still feels very risk-on. Oil keeps drifting lower toward $90, rates are calming down a bit, and equities  do not seem to care much about the geopolitical noise for now.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.03 (-1bp), US 10Y 4.47 (-2bp) — mild flattening, yields easing again as oil pulls back.  
USD: DXY 99.06 (-0.08%) — dollar still holding strong overall.  
FX: EURUSD +0.17%, USDJPY basically flat, USDCNH slightly lower — FX still very calm, except JPY continues acting a bit independently from rates.  
Equities: SPX +0.61%, VIX 17 — equities still near highs with almost no real fear bid.  
Commodities: Oil 91.0 (-2.7%), Gold -0.6% — oil keeps cooling as Hormuz reopening expectations continue improving.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is increasingly treating the Iran situation as something manageable rather than a systemic shock. Every time peace-talk headlines improve, oil level comes back down and risk sentiment improves immediately.

What matters more now is central banks, Europe is already more hawkish, Australia and New Zealand are tightening too, while the Fed still looks relatively patient compared to others.

Another interesting factor: even with lower oil today, central bankers keep repeating the same message — they are worried about second-round inflation effects, not just oil itself.

Risk  
The market still feels kind of complacent. If oil suddenly reverses back toward $100 or central banks become more openly hawkish, bonds will probably move first again — and eventually equities may have to react too.
