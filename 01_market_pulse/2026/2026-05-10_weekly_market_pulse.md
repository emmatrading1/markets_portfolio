Week of May 4–9, 2026

The main story this week was partial relief. Oil stayed high, but the market got some signs that Hormuz might not remain completely blocked.

Energy

Oil pulled back from the highs, ending closer to the mid-90s.

Still elevated, but the first LNG shipment through Hormuz changed the tone a bit.  
Not solved, but less one-way stress.

Rates

Rates stayed volatile.

The market is still not pricing easy cuts. If energy inflation lasts, central banks may need to stay tight or even hike.

Equities and volatility

Equities stayed strong.

VIX stayed around 17, so investors are clearly not panicking. The market is still willing to buy risk when oil falls.

FX

Dollar weakened overall.

No real safe-haven demand. USDJPY remains important because of yen/intervention dynamics.

Macro / flows

The energy shock is spreading beyond oil: gas, sulphur, fertilisers and industrial inputs matter too.

What to watch next week

• Oil below or above $100  
• More ships crossing Hormuz  
• Fed reaction to inflation  
• Any signs of supply-chain stress  
• Whether equities keep ignoring macro risk
