# Market Pulse 2026-03-10

Driver  
Trump signalled the Iran conflict could end “very soon”, easing fears of prolonged disruption to Middle East energy flows and reducing the geopolitical risk premium that had pushed oil close to $120.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.37% (-23bps), US 10Y 4.19% (+2bps) — bull steepening as front-end yields fall on reduced near-term tightening fears while the long end remains supported by inflation uncertainty linked to energy prices.  
USD: DXY 99.17 (-0.14%) — slight dollar weakness as safe-haven demand fades with improving risk sentiment.  
FX: EURUSD 1.158 (+0.17%), USDJPY 158.34 (-0.05%), USDCNH 6.91 (-0.17%) — mild USD softening across majors while CNH remains relatively stable.  
Equities: SPX 6795 (+0.83%), VIX 23.60 (-7.45%) — risk appetite returning as geopolitical fears ease and investors unwind hedges.  
Commodities: Oil $86.37 — crude sharply lower from levels above $100 as markets price reduced disruption risk in the Strait of Hormuz; Gold higher on weaker USD and residual geopolitical hedging.

FT Insights  
Oil markets reversed part of their surge after Trump suggested the conflict with Iran could end soon, prompting traders to unwind part of the geopolitical risk premium that had driven crude toward four-year highs.

Risk  
Any escalation involving Iranian retaliation or renewed disruption to Gulf energy infrastructure could quickly restore the geopolitical risk premium in oil and push inflation expectations higher again.
