# Market Pulse 2026-05-01

Driver  
Oil is still the main driver, but today the market is handling it better. Crude pulled back from the extreme intraday move, while equities stayed supported by strong tech/AI earnings. So the setup is still inflation risk in the background, but risk appetite has not broken.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.88 (+~1bp), US 10Y 4.37 (flat) — mild bear flattening, mostly front-end policy risk.  
USD: DXY 98.2 (+0.1%) — slightly firmer, but not a strong safe-haven move.  
FX: EURUSD −0.10%, USDJPY +0.27%, USDCNH flat — FX still quiet, except USDJPY moving with yield/intervention dynamics.  
Equities: SPX +0.29%, VIX 17.0 (+0.6%) — equities still constructive, vol very low given the oil backdrop.  
Commodities: Oil 102.5 (−2.8%), Gold flat — oil still high, but the pullback helps sentiment.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The main tension is clear: oil is still elevated, but equities are being carried by tech and AI. The market is not ignoring the energy shock, but it is choosing to focus on earnings and AI capex for now.

Central banks are also in a difficult position. ECB and BoE held rates, but both are leaving the door open to hikes if energy inflation starts feeding into wages and expectations.

Risk  
The market still looks resilient, but it is not a clean risk-on setup.

If oil keeps moving lower, equities can probably keep grinding higher. But if crude moves back toward the highs and stays there, the current calm becomes harder to justify. The risk is that rates reprice first, then equities follow later.
