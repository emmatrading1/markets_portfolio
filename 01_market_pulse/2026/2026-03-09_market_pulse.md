# Market Pulse 2026-03-09

Driver  
Escalation of the Middle East conflict and disruption to oil flows through the Strait of Hormuz pushing crude above $100 and triggering renewed inflation fears.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.60% (+1.29%), US 10Y 4.17% (+0.85%) — front-end yields rising faster as markets price higher inflation risk and fewer near-term Fed rate cuts.  
USD: DXY 99.30 (+0.45%) — dollar supported by higher yields and safe-haven demand.  
FX: EURUSD 1.156 (-0.51%), USDJPY 158.42 (+0.42%), USDCNH 6.92 (+0.24%) — broad USD strength as higher US yields attract capital.  
Equities: SPX 6740 (-1.33%), VIX 31.02 (+5.9%) — risk-off tone with investors increasing hedging amid geopolitical uncertainty.  
Commodities: Oil ~$100 (+10.3%) as supply disruptions intensify; Gold ~$5103 (-1.3%) as stronger USD offsets safe-haven demand.

FT Insights  
Oil prices surged as attacks on Gulf energy infrastructure and halted tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz threaten roughly a fifth of global oil and LNG supply. Several Gulf producers have begun cutting production while exports remain constrained, embedding a geopolitical risk premium into crude markets.

Risk  
A prolonged disruption to energy exports from the Gulf could push oil significantly higher, reinforcing inflation pressures and forcing markets to further reprice the outlook for Fed policy and global growth.
