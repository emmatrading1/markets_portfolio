# Weekend Macro Brief — June 20 2026

Driver  
The market stayed in post-ceasefire mode. Oil kept falling, equities remained supported, and volatility stayed mostly contained, but bonds and AI stocks showed that the relief trade is not risk-free.

Macro Developments  
This week moved from deal relief to consolidation.

Early week: the US-Iran deal triggered a strong risk-on move.  
Mid-week: oil kept easing, but the market started questioning how quickly Hormuz can really normalise.  
End of week: the dollar strengthened, rates stayed important, and AI/SpaceX volatility returned.

So overall: the energy shock is fading, but the market is not fully back to normal.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil fell from around $80 toward the low/mid-$70s, but flows, ships and inventories are still not fully normal.

Rates: Yields eased at first, then stayed elevated as markets kept pricing a more cautious Fed.

FX: Dollar strengthened back above DXY 100, supported by US exceptionalism and resilient growth.

Equities: SPX stayed high, but the move became less clean as SpaceX and AI names showed valuation pressure.

Cross-asset: Lower oil supports risk assets, but higher-for-longer rates and fragile energy logistics keep the setup more complicated.

Market Narrative  
The market is asking: “Is this a clean reset after the Iran shock?”

For now, the answer is: partly, but not fully. Lower oil helps inflation, consumers and risk appetite.

But the physical energy system still needs time to recover, and the bond market is more cautious than equities.

Key Points to Watch  
• Oil around the low/mid-$70s  
• Hormuz traffic and shipping confidence  
• Fed communication under Warsh  
• Treasury yields  
• Dollar strength above 100  
• AI / SpaceX volatility  
• VIX staying below or moving back above 20
