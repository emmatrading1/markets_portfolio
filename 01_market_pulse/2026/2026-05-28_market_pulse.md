# Market Pulse 2026-05-28

Driver  
Oil is still the main macro driver, but the market is now much calmer. Crude is near $90, which helps the risk sentiment, even though rates are moving slightly higher again.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.06 (+2bp), US 10Y 4.50 (+1bp) — slight bear flattening, front end reacting more to inflation/central bank risk.  
USD: DXY 99.35 (+0.13%) — dollar still firm, supported by higher rates and uncertainty.  
FX: EURUSD slightly lower, USDJPY almost flat, USDCNH flat — currencies are calm overall.  
Equities: SPX flat around 7520, VIX 16.7 (+2.3%) — equities still very strong, with no real protection demand.  
Commodities: Oil 90.3 (+1%), Gold -1.2% — oil is still high but much less stressful than above $100.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The key point is that equities are still choosing the positive story: AI, semiconductors and earnings. Even with the ECB warnings and the stretched valuations, stocks are not really pricing a financial-stability scare.

Risk  
The risk is that markets are becoming too comfortable. Oil has calmed, but rates, debt concerns and geopolitical risk are still there. If oil rebounds or yields push higher again, equities may have to react.
