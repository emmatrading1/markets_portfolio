# Market Pulse 2026-04-09

Driver  
Markets are stabilising after yesterday’s sharp de-risking move, with the main driver now shifting from pure de-escalation to uncertainty around whether the ceasefire will hold, keeping oil supported and volatility slightly bid.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.78 (−1bp), US 10Y 4.29 (−1bp) — mild bull flattening continues, but momentum is slowing, suggesting markets are pausing after aggressively pricing easing.<br>
USD: DXY 99.05 (+0.05%) — dollar broadly stable, holding near 99 as positioning consolidates.<br>
FX: EURUSD +0.04%, USDJPY +0.30%, USDCNH +0.09% — muted FX moves overall, with slight USD strength vs JPY reflecting stabilising yields.<br>
Equities: SPX +2.51%, VIX 21.52 (+2.28%) — strong equity rally continues, but rising VIX signals ongoing demand for hedging despite risk-on positioning.<br>
Commodities: Oil 97.63 (+1.18%), Gold +0.25% — oil rebounds as supply uncertainty persists, while gold remains supported by residual macro and geopolitical risk.

WSJ/FT Insights  
FT highlights that despite the ceasefire, uncertainty remains elevated, with OECD warning that energy-driven inflation could persist and weigh on growth, especially if policy responses (e.g. fuel subsidies) distort incentives and fiscal stability. At the same time, US crude exports are surging as Asia replaces disrupted Middle Eastern supply, reinforcing the US role as swing supplier but also keeping upward pressure on global prices.  
WSJ notes that markets are transitioning from panic to positioning, with equities rallying on relief while oil remains structurally elevated. The key shift is that even with de-escalation, inflation risks remain sticky, complicating the Fed’s ability to cut.

Risk  
Markets are now caught between relief and residual uncertainty. If the ceasefire holds, focus will shift back to growth and policy, but elevated oil levels could keep inflation sticky and delay rate cuts. Any renewed disruption in the Strait of Hormuz could quickly push oil higher again, reversing the current risk-on move and reigniting volatility.
