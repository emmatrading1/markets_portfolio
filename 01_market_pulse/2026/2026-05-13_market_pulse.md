# Market Pulse 2026-05-13

Driver  
Oil is still the background driver, but today the main focus is inflation. US CPI came in hotter, which confirms that the energy shock is starting to pass through beyond just crude. The market reaction is still controlled, but the rates story is becoming less comfortable.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.98 (−1bp), US 10Y 4.46 (flat) — slight bull steepening on the day, but yields remain high. The bigger point is that the curve is still pricing sticky inflation / higher-for-longer risk.  
USD: DXY 98.5 (+0.2%) — dollar firmer, helped by inflation and fewer reasons to price Fed cuts.  
FX: EURUSD −0.27%, USDJPY +0.11%, USDCNH flat — FX still quiet, USDJPY slightly higher with the dollar/yield backdrop.  
Equities: SPX −0.16%, VIX 17.9 (−0.8%) — equities slightly softer, but no stress. VIX below 18 still shows very limited demand for protection.  
Commodities: Oil 101.7 (slightly lower), Gold −0.4% — oil still above $100, gold softer as real-rate pressure remains.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The important point today is that disinflation is fading. Inflation is no longer only an oil-price story: fuel, airfares, food and some goods are starting to show pass-through. India raising gold tariffs also shows the same broader issue in another form: energy-importing economies are trying to protect currencies and current accounts.

Risk  
The risk is that markets stay too calm while the inflation story gets worse. Equities are not breaking, VIX is low, and oil is slightly off yesterday’s level, so the surface looks stable. But if inflation keeps moving higher while oil stays above $100, the Fed has much less room to sound dovish. That is where rates could become the real problem for equities later.
