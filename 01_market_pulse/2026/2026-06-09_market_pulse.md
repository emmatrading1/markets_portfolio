# Market Pulse 2026-06-09

Driver  
The market is still unstable after the AI sell-off, but is also not fully risk-off. Oil is below $90, rates eased, and equities only slipped slightly. Main factor is volatility: energy looks calmer, but AI and Fed uncertainty are keeping market nervous.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.12 (-4bp), US 10Y 4.52 (-5bp) — yields eased after the recent bond stress.  
USD: DXY 99.94 (-0.06%) — dollar slightly softer, but still close to 100.  
FX: EURUSD slightly lower, USDJPY slightly lower, USDCNH flat — FX is still calm overall.  
Equities: SPX -0.26%, VIX 19.9 (+5.0%) — stocks softer and protection demand still elevated.  
Commodities: Oil 88.7 (-2.8%), Gold -1.6% — oil relief continues, but gold did not catch a safety bid.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The oil market is still avoiding the feared summer crunch. China’s lower imports and inventory drawdowns are helping keep crude below $100, even with Hormuz still disrupted.

But the stability is fragile. The AI trade rebounded after the sell-off, yet the FT points to a market now set up for more volatility because positioning is crowded and rates are uncertain.

Risk  
The risk is that markets are confusing resilience with resolution. Oil is lower for now, but inventories are being drained, and if China comes back to the market or the Fed turns more hawkish, volatility could rise again quickly.
