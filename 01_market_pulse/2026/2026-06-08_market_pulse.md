# Market Pulse 2026-06-08

Driver  
The market is becoming a bit more defensive. Yields are higher, oil is higher and volatility is rising, all  putting pressure on equities, especially AI and semiconductors. Investors are starting to question whether the risk-on rally went too far, too fast.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.19 (+4bp), US 10Y 4.57 (+4bp) — bear flattening, with the front end still leading higher.  
USD: DXY 100.15 (+0.08%) — dollar remains firm as higher rates support demand for USD assets.  
FX: EURUSD lower, USDJPY lower, USDCNH lower — FX moves remain relatively contained despite the macro shift.  
Equities: SPX 7,384 (-2.6%), VIX 20.0 (+30% vs recent lows) — risk appetite is fading and demand for protection is rising.  
Commodities: Oil 94.8 (+5.1%), Gold -0.9% — oil is back near $95 as Middle East tensions persist, while gold is giving back some recent gains.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is increasingly focused on the possibility that rates stay higher for longer. A stronger-than-expected US jobs report pushed investors to price a greater chance of Fed tightening rather than easing.

At the same time, the AI trade is showing signs of fatigue. Semiconductor stocks led a sharp sell-off across Asia, while concerns are growing that higher rates, expensive valuations and a wave of large equity issuance could pressure the sector.

Risk  
The risk is that this becomes more than a simple correction. If oil keeps rising, inflation remains elevated and central banks stay hawkish, markets may have to reprice both growth expectations and AI valuations at the same time.
