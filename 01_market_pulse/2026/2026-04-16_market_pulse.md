# Market Pulse 2026-04-16

Driver  
Markets are stabilising on the surface, but oil pushing back up shows the underlying story is still supply-driven — less panic, not less risk.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 3.78 (+2bp), US 10Y 4.32 (+3bp) — mild bear steepening, long-end still drifting higher.  
USD: DXY 98.21 (+0.16%) — dollar slightly firmer, small return of safe-haven tone.  
FX: EURUSD −0.03%, USDJPY −0.01%, USDCNH flat — very quiet, no strong directional conviction.  
Equities: SPX +0.26%, VIX 17.9 (−1.3%) — equities grinding up, vol compressing further.  
Commodities: Oil 93.2 (+2.0%), Gold −0.1% — oil rebounds, signalling supply still tight; gold flat.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The calm in futures is a bit misleading — physical oil markets remain tight, with ongoing disruption around the Strait and strong demand for alternative supply. At the same time, Gulf sovereigns are raising debt post-shock, showing real economic pressure beneath the surface. Structurally, markets are also more fragile, with high leverage sitting in hedge funds and trading firms, which could amplify any volatility spike.

Risk  
This still feels like a pause rather than a reset. Vol is low and equities are holding up, but oil moving higher again is a reminder the supply issue isn’t solved. As long as flows stay constrained, inflation risk stays alive. If things remain calm, markets drift higher. If oil or geopolitics flare up again, the move back into volatility will be quick.
