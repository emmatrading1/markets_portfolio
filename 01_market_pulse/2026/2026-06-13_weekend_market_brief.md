# Weekend Macro Brief — June 13 2026

Driver  
The market shifted from oil stress to deal relief. Oil fell sharply, equities recovered, and bonds gained, but AI funding and inflation risk remain the deeper macro story.

Macro Developments  
This week moved from volatility to relief.

Early week: tech and AI stocks corrected as rates and positioning pressure hit the market.  
Mid-week: US inflation rose, but core inflation stayed contained enough for the Fed to wait.  
End of week: oil fell on hopes of a US-Iran deal, helping equities and bonds recover.

So overall: the market is calmer, but the setup is still fragile.

Transmission  

Energy: Oil fell toward the mid-$80s as Hormuz flows improved and deal hopes increased.

Rates: Yields eased as markets pushed back the timing of expected rate hikes.

FX: Dollar stayed firm, but the oil relief reduced some inflation pressure.

Equities: SPX recovered and volatility eased, helped by lower oil and renewed AI appetite.

Cross-asset: Oil relief helped risk assets, but AI equity supply and central banks still matter.

Market Narrative  
The market is asking: “Is the energy shock fading?”

For now, investors are leaning yes. Lower oil and improving Hormuz flows support risk appetite.

But the AI story is changing too: Big Tech now needs capital, not just generates it.

• Whether Hormuz flows keep improving
