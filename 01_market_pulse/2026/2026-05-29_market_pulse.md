# Market Pulse 2026-05-29

Driver  
Oil is still easing, and that keeps the market in risk-on mode. Crude is now below $90, so equities are treating the energy shock as manageable for now.

Transmission  
Rates: US 2Y 4.01 (-1bp), US 10Y 4.45 (flat) — mild bull steepening, but yields remain high.  
USD: DXY 98.9 (-0.1%) — dollar softer as oil pressure fades.  
FX: EURUSD slightly higher, USDJPY flat, USDCNH lower — broader FX still calm.  
Equities: SPX +0.36%, VIX 15.3 (-2.8%) — strong risk tone, very little protection demand.  
Commodities: Oil 87.8 (-0.8%), Gold +1.3% — oil keeps falling, but gold is still bid.

WSJ/FT Insights  
The market is leaning into the positive oil story: lower crude, lower volatility, stronger equities. But the FT point is important: inventories and reserves are being drawn down, so the current calm may not be permanent.

Risk  
The risk is that markets price a clean de-escalation too early. If oil jumps again over summer or financial stability concerns return, equities could look too relaxed.
