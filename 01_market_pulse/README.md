# Market Pulse

This folder contains short daily notes on markets.

Each note is based on a couple of articles (FT, WSJ, Bloomberg) or macro releases from the day. The goal is to stay close to what is moving markets and to understand how news moves prices.

Most of my notes look at things like:

- macro events
- central bank expectations
- moves in rates, FX, equities or commodities
- and shifts in market narratives/positioning

Weekend notes are a bit different and usually summarize the main themes from the week.

## How a note is built

**Driver** (what moved the markets) → **Transmission** (rates, USD, FX, equities, commodities, with levels) → **Risk** (what could change the picture)
